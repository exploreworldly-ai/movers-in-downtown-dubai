High-rise move planning

Apartment movers in Downtown Dubai

Apartment moves in Downtown Dubai can involve building-specific procedures. Management may require advance registration, a moving permit or NOC, a reserved service lift, and approved loading or parking arrangements. Check the rules with your building management rather than assuming the same process applies everywhere.

Confirm the access arrangements at both the current and new property. Finish packing before the agreed moving window and keep any required approvals available on the day.

Read the apartment moving and NOC guide →

Before your apartment move

  • Confirm moving requirements with building management.
  • Ask whether an NOC or moving permit is required.
  • Reserve the service lift if necessary.
  • Confirm loading and parking access.
  • Finish packing before the booked moving window.

Requirements vary by building. Confirm current procedures with the relevant management office.

Business relocations

Office movers in Downtown Dubai

An office move needs a plan for people, equipment, documents, and access at both locations. Clear labels and an agreed sequence can help the team identify where workstations and equipment belong after delivery.

Protect computers and monitors, organize files, and check building access rules before moving day. Where appropriate, consider scheduling outside normal working hours, subject to team availability and building requirements.

Office move priorities

  • Label desks, workstations, and equipment.
  • Protect computers, monitors, and accessories.
  • Pack files in an organized, traceable way.
  • Confirm loading access and lift arrangements.
  • Agree the move sequence and timing in advance.
